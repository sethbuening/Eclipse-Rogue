Eclipse-Rogue is a vampire survivors roguelite which includes mining in the same way as Deep Rock Galactic: Survivor.
Though the game is 2D, it emulates 3D somewhat through both the perspective and the effects applied when mining tiles.

The player can fight enemies through the automatic activation of their abilities.

Killing enough enemies will eventually cause the player to level up.
The player may be given the opportunity to either add an ability, or just to upgrade a current ability.

Mined resources can then be used to increase the power of upgrades on leveling up.
The player may save their resources and just take a base upgrade, or they might spend as much as possible to maximize the stat improvement provided by that upgrade.
